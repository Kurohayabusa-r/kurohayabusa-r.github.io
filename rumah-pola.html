Visit Rumah Pola ↗

The brief

Rumah Pola produces uniforms, bags, and custom merchandise in Jakarta. Its website needs to introduce the business, show the range of products it makes, and help prospective customers start a conversation.

I developed a refreshed company profile around product photography, selected work, and direct contact links, with a separate gallery for exploring collections.

What I built

Company & product overview

A homepage covering the business, product categories, selected work, customer logos, and contact information.

Filterable product gallery

Collections organized by category, with photo dialogs, previous and next controls, and keyboard navigation.

Mobile navigation

A responsive layout with a compact navigation menu and product collections that adapt to smaller screens.

Direct contact paths

WhatsApp, email, and social links make it straightforward to move from viewing work to discussing an order.

Implementation

Static pages, focused interactions

HTML and CSS provide the page structure and responsive layout. JavaScript adds category filters, the photo viewer, mobile navigation, and motion enhancements.

Image preparation

The publishing workflow prepares WebP product photos for the gallery. Page metadata, image descriptions, and structured business information accompany the visual content.

Cloudflare Pages

The website is deployed as a static site on Cloudflare Pages, with the homepage and gallery linked throughout the experience.
